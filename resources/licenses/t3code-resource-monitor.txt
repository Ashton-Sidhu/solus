solus-resource-monitor is the T3 Code resource monitor
(https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code, native/resource-monitor, commit dd5f98ee),
used without change except for its crate name. Solus's server client and user
interface are original, and the load balancing rule is adapted from T3 Code.

The compiled monitor also includes crates under their own licenses: sysinfo,
objc2, and zmij (MIT), and serde, serde_json, itoa, libc, memchr, bitflags,
dispatch2, and the windows crates (each offers MIT among its license choices).
